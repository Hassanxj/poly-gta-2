A top-down open-city crime game in the style of the original GTA (1997), built for
[make.fun](https://make.fun) — bounty [gta6-01](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01).

You are a little guy in blue in the city of Solmar — a pixelated 3D voxel
character with a blocky blue jacket, a white pixel belt buckle and a pale
pixel collar, spiky black hair and a face you can
read at a glance, like every pedestrian and police officer in the city.
Walk the streets, steal cars, dodge the police, and run three jobs — First Wheels (steal a car, drive to the docks),
Beach Run (deliver a package to the pier) and Heat Wave (escape to the safehouse).
Wanted stars rise when you steal and shoot, and fall when you lay low. Traffic,
pedestrians, police chases, shooting, car wrecks and explosions — all in plain
Canvas 2D, no network, no library.

## How to play

```
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.

- WASD / arrow keys — walk and drive
- E — steal / enter a car (and get out again)
- F or click — shoot (on foot)
- H — horn
- P — pause

Your best score is saved in the browser.
